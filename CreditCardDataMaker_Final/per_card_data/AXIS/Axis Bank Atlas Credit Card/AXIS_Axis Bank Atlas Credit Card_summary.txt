Unlock additional benefits in gold and platinum tier memberships.

Each tier comes with base earn of 2 EDGE Miles per ₹100 spent and accelerated earn of 5 EDGE Miles per ₹100 spent on TRAVEL EDGE portal, direct airlines and direct hotel spends.
Each tier comes with milestone and annual benefits in the form of EDGE Miles that can be redeemed.
